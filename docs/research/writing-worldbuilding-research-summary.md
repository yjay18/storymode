# Writing and Worldbuilding Research Summary

## Current evidence status

No external prose/worldbuilding sources were retrieved during setup. The initial
policy comes from the supplied product brief: per-world style bibles, concrete sensory
and material details, faction/cultural language, readable core skill labels, and
negative examples that suppress generic prose.

## Research protocol for a campaign

Before generating a style bible, collect at least three user-approved reference
examples when lawful and available. For each, record URL or user-provided provenance,
access date, no more than a short compliant excerpt, and original notes on:

- narrative distance, sentence rhythm, dialogue density, and exposition pattern;
- recurring sensory channels, materials, lighting, maintenance, and scarcity;
- naming morphology for places, institutions, objects, and enemies;
- how culture/faction appears through behavior and environment rather than lore dumps;
- traits to emulate abstractly and traits/phrases to avoid.

Then write original positive and negative examples. Never ask a runtime model to
imitate a living author or reproduce supplied source passages. Convert references into
high-level traits and user-selected campaign rules.

## Insufficient evidence behavior

If fewer than three suitable examples can be retrieved, say which searches/sources
failed and ask the user for references. A quick campaign may proceed with explicitly
generic original defaults, labeled as such for review; it must not invent citations.
